Year of Establishment: 2014

The Manufacturer of Mobile Light Towers

Established in the year 2014, we "Ariselux Equipments Private Limited" (previously known as "Arise Construction Equipments") are the leading manufacturer of Mobile Lighting Towers, Lighting Systems, and Portable Emergency Illumination solutions.

We direct all our activities to cater to the expectations of customers by providing them excellent quality products as per their gratification. Moreover, we follow moral business policies and crystal-pure transparency in all our transactions to maintain healthy, long-lasting relations with our clients.

Our towers offer powerful illumination, high fuel efficiency, and structural durability, ensuring peak performance even in the harshest environments. With a persistent focus on innovation and quality, we deliver both standard and custom lighting solutions to meet diverse industrial and government needs locally and globally.
