Our Story

Illuminating The World With Powerful Solutions

Ariselux (Arise Equipments) is a leading manufacturer and supplier of mobile lighting towers, dedicated to providing powerful, reliable, and innovative illumination solutions for industries worldwide.

With years of experience in precision machining and high-quality fabrication, we consistently innovate to design lighting towers of premium quality and aesthetic appeal. Our products are engineered to perform in the most demanding conditions — from dusty construction sites to remote mining operations.

We take pride in our commitment to quality, customer satisfaction, and continuous innovation. Every lighting tower that leaves our facility undergoes rigorous testing to ensure it meets the highest standards of performance and safety.
