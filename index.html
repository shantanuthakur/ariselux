Engineering Excellence

What's Unique About Our LED Technology?

Every Ariselux mobile light tower is engineered using cutting-edge optics and rugged mechanical standards designed to survive the harshest industrial and outdoor environments.

🛡️
Durable Aluminum Casting

Lamps are built with heavy-duty aluminum die-casting that guarantees robust impact and vibration protection during transport and rough site terrain.

💡
High CRI (>85 out of 100)

Equipped with high Color Rendering Index LED lamps for superior optical accuracy, high visibility, and safer nighttime working conditions.

📐
Advanced Directional Optics

Precision lens arrays deliver up to 5,000 to 10,000 m² of uniform illumination with an average brightness of 20 lux and zero wasted light bleed.

⏳
50,000+ Hours Lifespan

Industrial LED drivers and thermal dissipation modules offer an ultra-long operating life exceeding 50,000 hours with negligible maintenance.

Additional Standard Features Across All Towers

🏗️
Integrated Lifting Point

Heavy-duty central crane lifting eye for rapid site loading and deployment.

🚜
Forklift Pockets

Reinforced chassis slots for effortless transport with standard forklifts and telehandlers.

🚗
Foldable Adjustable Towbar

Straight and foldable towing hitches compatible with standard commercial and industrial vehicles.
